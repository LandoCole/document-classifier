Note 1. Organization and Liquidity

Quillon Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing oral therapies for fibrotic lung disease. The accompanying unaudited condensed financial statements have been prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP for interim financial information and Article 10 of Regulation S-X. The interim results are not necessarily indicative of the results to be expected for the year ending December 31, 2025, or any other period. The balance sheet at December 31, 2024 was derived from the audited financial statements included in the Company's Annual Report on Form 10-K filed on March 18, 2025.

The Company has incurred net losses since inception and had an accumulated deficit of $212.8 million at March 31, 2025. The Company had cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $55.4 million at March 31, 2025, which management believes is sufficient to fund operations into the first quarter of 2026, which is less than twelve months from the date these financial statements were issued. These conditions raise substantial doubt about the Company's ability to continue as a going concern.

Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview. We are developing QLT-114, an oral inhibitor, in a Phase 2b trial in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. We dosed the 240th patient in the trial in February 2025 and expect to report topline data in the fourth quarter of 2025.

Research and development expenses increased $3.6 million to $14.9 million for the three months ended March 31, 2025, primarily due to $2.4 million of higher clinical trial costs and $0.8 million of higher personnel costs. General and administrative expenses increased $0.7 million, due to higher professional fees and stock-based compensation.

Liquidity. Net cash used in operating activities was $17.5 million for the quarter. We have funded operations primarily through the sale of equity securities. In April 2025 we entered into an at-the-market sales agreement allowing sales of up to $40.0 million of common stock; no shares have been sold as of the date of this report.
