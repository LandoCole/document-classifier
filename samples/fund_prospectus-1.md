## FUND SUMMARY

The Calloway Street Large Cap Value Fund (the "Fund") seeks long-term capital appreciation, with current income as a secondary objective.

### Principal Investment Strategies

Under normal circumstances, the Fund invests at least 80% of its net assets, plus borrowings for investment purposes, in common stocks of large-capitalization companies. The Fund considers a company to be large-capitalization if its market capitalization at the time of purchase is within the range of the Large Cap Value Composite Index, which was between $2.1 billion and $612 billion as of December 31, 2024. The Adviser uses a bottom-up fundamental approach to select stocks it believes are trading below their intrinsic value, focusing on companies with strong balance sheets, durable free cash flow and shareholder-friendly capital allocation. The Fund may invest up to 15% of its net assets in securities of foreign issuers, including American Depositary Receipts. The Adviser may sell a stock when it reaches its estimated fair value, when the investment thesis has changed or when a better opportunity is identified.

### Principal Risks

You could lose money by investing in the Fund. An investment in the Fund is not a bank deposit and is not insured or guaranteed by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation or any other government agency.

- **Market Risk.** The value of the Fund's shares may fluctuate as stock markets rise and fall.
- **Value Investing Risk.** Undervalued stocks may not appreciate to the extent expected or may remain undervalued for long periods.
- **Large-Cap Company Risk.** Large companies may be unable to attain the high growth rates of smaller companies and may fall out of favor.
- **Sector Risk.** As of the most recent fiscal year end, the Fund had significant exposure to the financials and health care sectors.
- **Foreign Securities Risk.** Foreign investments involve currency, political and liquidity risks.
- **Management Risk.** The Adviser's judgments about the attractiveness of securities may prove to be incorrect.

### Purchase and Sale of Fund Shares

The minimum initial investment is $2,500 for Class A and Class C shares ($1,000 for IRAs) and $1,000,000 for Institutional Class shares. The minimum subsequent investment is $100. You may purchase or redeem shares on any day the New York Stock Exchange is open, by mail, by telephone at 1-800-555-0178, through a financial intermediary or by wire. Redemption requests are priced at the net asset value next calculated after receipt in good order.

### Tax Information

The Fund's distributions are taxable as ordinary income or capital gains, unless you are investing through a tax-deferred arrangement such as a 401(k) plan or an individual retirement account. Withdrawals from tax-deferred accounts may be taxable.

### Payments to Broker-Dealers and Other Financial Intermediaries

If you purchase the Fund through a broker-dealer or other financial intermediary, the Fund and its related companies may pay the intermediary for the sale of Fund shares and related services. These payments may create a conflict of interest by influencing the broker-dealer or other intermediary and your salesperson to recommend the Fund over another investment.
