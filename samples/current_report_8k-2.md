Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

Departure of Chief Financial Officer. On October 14, 2025, Sorrel Ridge Energy Corp. (the "Company") announced that Gregory L. Tallman, the Company's Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, informed the Board of Directors of his decision to retire from the Company effective December 31, 2025. Mr. Tallman's decision did not result from any disagreement with the Company on any matter relating to the Company's operations, policies or practices, including its financial reporting.

Appointment of Chief Financial Officer. On October 14, 2025, the Board appointed Elena V. Marchetti, age 47, as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, effective January 1, 2026. Ms. Marchetti has served as the Company's Senior Vice President, Finance and Treasurer since 2021. Previously, she held finance positions of increasing responsibility at Delmar Pipeline Partners and at a Big Four accounting firm. There are no arrangements or understandings between Ms. Marchetti and any other person pursuant to which she was selected as an officer, and she has no family relationship with any director or executive officer of the Company. There are no transactions between Ms. Marchetti and the Company required to be disclosed under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K.

Compensatory arrangements. In connection with her appointment, the Compensation Committee approved an annual base salary of $540,000, a target annual bonus of 90% of base salary and a long-term incentive award for 2026 with a target grant value of $1,500,000, consisting of 60% performance share units and 40% restricted stock units that vest ratably over three years. Ms. Marchetti will also enter into the Company's standard executive severance agreement.

Transition. Mr. Tallman will remain as an advisor through June 30, 2026 and will receive $45,000 per month during that period. His unvested restricted stock units will continue to vest in accordance with their terms.
