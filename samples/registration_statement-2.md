PRELIMINARY PROSPECTUS -- SUBJECT TO COMPLETION

5,100,000 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK

This prospectus relates to the resale by the selling stockholders identified in this prospectus of up to 5,100,000 shares of our common stock, consisting of 4,250,000 outstanding shares issued in a private placement completed on April 8, 2025 and 850,000 shares issuable upon exercise of warrants issued in that placement at an exercise price of $1.60 per share.

We are not selling any shares of common stock under this prospectus and will not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders. We will receive proceeds if the warrants are exercised for cash. We have agreed to bear the expenses of registration, other than selling commissions.

Our common stock is quoted on the OTCQB under the symbol "THMF." On May 29, 2025, the last reported sale price was $1.87 per share.

Investing in our common stock involves a high degree of risk. See "Risk Factors" beginning on page 5.

PROSPECTUS SUMMARY

Tern Harbor Marine Fuels, Inc. distributes marine diesel, lubricants and fuel-additive products to commercial fishing fleets, tug operators and small ports along the Gulf and southeastern Atlantic coasts. We operated 6 fuel terminals and 11 bunkering barges as of March 31, 2025. For the year ended December 31, 2024 we generated revenue of $38.2 million and a net loss of $1.4 million.
