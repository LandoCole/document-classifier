### Investment Thesis

We initiate coverage of Cascade Grid Utilities with an Overweight rating and a 12-month price target of $48. Cascade is a pure-play regulated electric and gas utility serving 3.1 million customers across Oregon, Washington and Idaho. We see three reasons the stock should re-rate over the next 12 months:

1. **Visible rate base growth of 8.5%.** Management's $14.2 billion five-year capital plan, 82% of which is already included in rate cases or approved riders, supports rate base growth well above the sector median of 6.5%.
2. **Constructive regulation.** The Oregon commission's recent order authorized a 9.9% return on equity on a 50% equity layer, and Washington's multi-year plan reduces regulatory lag to under six months.
3. **Valuation dislocation.** Shares trade at 14.6x our 2027E EPS versus regulated peers at 17.1x, a discount we think reflects wildfire headlines more than fundamentals.

### Balance Sheet and Credit

Leverage is the bear case, so we spend time on it. Funds from operations to debt is expected to average 14.5% through 2028. Cascade's senior secured first mortgage bonds are rated A- with a stable outlook by Calloway Credit Analytics, and the holding company's unsecured debt is rated BBB with a stable outlook. Calloway's most recent report, dated August 2026, said the ratings could be lowered if FFO to debt falls below 13% for a sustained period. We do not model a downgrade, but we note that an unsecured downgrade to BBB- would raise the cost of new holding company debt by roughly 30 basis points, or about $0.02 of EPS per year. We think this is manageable. Cascade has $1.1 billion of equity forward contracts that settle through 2027, which covers about 70% of the equity need in the capital plan.

### Risks to Our Rating and Price Target

- **Wildfire liability.** An ignition event attributed to Cascade equipment could produce losses beyond insurance. Cascade carries $850 million in wildfire coverage and is a participant in the Oregon wildfire fund.
- **Regulatory.** A less favorable order in the pending Washington rate case (decision due March 2027) could reduce allowed ROE by 25 to 50 basis points.
- **Interest rates.** A 100 basis point rise in the 10-year Treasury yield would cut our valuation by about $4 per share.
- **Execution** on the 740 MW solar-plus-storage buildout.

### Company Description

Cascade Grid Utilities, headquartered in Portland, Oregon, owns Cascade Electric, Cascade Gas and Cascade Renewables. It generates approximately 91% of earnings from regulated operations.
