## Item 4. Purpose of Transaction

Item 4 is amended and supplemented as follows:

The Reporting Persons acquired the shares of Common Stock because they believe the shares are undervalued and represent an attractive investment opportunity.

On September 29, 2026, Larkin Value Partners, LP delivered a letter (the "Letter") to the Board of Directors of the Issuer. In the Letter, the Reporting Persons stated their belief that the Issuer's recent underperformance relative to peers reflects capital allocation decisions and a cost structure that can be improved. The Reporting Persons proposed that the Board:

1. Initiate a formal review of strategic alternatives for the Issuer's Outdoor Living segment, including a sale or spin-off;
2. Commit to reducing selling, general and administrative expense to 18% of net sales by fiscal 2028, from 22.4% in the most recent fiscal year;
3. Add two independent directors with retail and consumer products operating experience, in consultation with the Reporting Persons; and
4. Tie at least half of named executive officer long-term incentive compensation to return on invested capital.

On October 1, 2026, representatives of the Reporting Persons spoke with the Issuer's Chair of the Board, Margaret Delacroix-Hines, regarding the Letter. The Reporting Persons intend to continue engaging with the Issuer's Board and management. If the Board does not engage constructively, the Reporting Persons may nominate candidates for election to the Board at the 2027 annual meeting of stockholders, seek to call a special meeting, submit proposals, communicate with other stockholders, or take other actions.

The Reporting Persons reserve the right to change their intentions with respect to any and all matters referred to in Item 4, including to purchase additional shares or to dispose of some or all of their shares, depending on market conditions, the Issuer's business and prospects, and other factors.

Except as set forth above, the Reporting Persons do not have any present plans or proposals that relate to or would result in any of the matters in clauses (a) through (j) of Item 4 of Schedule 13D.
