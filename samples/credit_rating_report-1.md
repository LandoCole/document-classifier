# Aldermoor Industrial Corp.: Long-Term Issuer Rating Affirmed at 'BBB+'; Outlook Stable

## Summary of Rating Action

Calloway Credit Analytics (Calloway) has affirmed Aldermoor Industrial Corp.'s (Aldermoor) Long-Term Issuer Default Rating (IDR) at 'BBB+' with a Stable Outlook. Calloway has also affirmed the ratings on Aldermoor's senior unsecured notes at 'BBB+' and its commercial paper program at 'F2'.

| Rating | Action | Rating | Prior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-Term Issuer Default Rating | Affirmed | BBB+ ; Outlook Stable | BBB+ ; Outlook Stable |
| Senior Unsecured Notes | Affirmed | BBB+ | BBB+ |
| Short-Term Issuer Rating | Affirmed | F2 | F2 |
| Commercial Paper | Affirmed | F2 | F2 |
| Syndicated Revolving Credit Facility | Affirmed | BBB+ | BBB+ |

The affirmation reflects Aldermoor's leading positions in industrial pumps, valves and flow control, its diversified end-markets, a solid record of free cash flow generation and conservative financial policies. Offsetting factors include exposure to cyclical oil and gas and chemical end-markets, a recent acquisition that raised leverage temporarily, and a lower-than-peer share of recurring aftermarket revenue.

Calloway expects Aldermoor to reduce EBITDA leverage to 2.4x by fiscal 2028 from 2.9x at the end of fiscal 2026 (June), supported by roughly $450 million of cumulative free cash flow, which the company has indicated will be directed to debt reduction before any resumption of large share repurchases.

## Key Rating Drivers

**Leading Market Positions with Diversification.** Aldermoor generated $6.8 billion of revenue in fiscal 2026. Approximately 38% of revenue came from process industries (chemicals, refining, oil and gas), 24% from water and wastewater, 19% from power generation, 12% from food and pharmaceutical and 7% from other sectors. No customer accounted for more than 3% of revenue. The company holds the first or second market share in six of its eight principal product lines in North America.

**Elevated Leverage Following Acquisition.** In March 2026 Aldermoor acquired Tessel Fluid Dynamics for $1.35 billion, funded with $900 million of new senior notes and cash on hand. Pro forma EBITDA leverage rose to 3.1x at closing from 2.2x. Calloway views the integration risk as moderate; Tessel brings $210 million in aftermarket revenue and will raise the aftermarket share from 27% to 29% of sales.

**Strong Cash Flow.** Funds from operations (FFO) were $882 million in fiscal 2026 and FFO-adjusted leverage was 3.2x. Free cash flow margin after dividends averaged 5.1% over the last four years, among the strongest in the peer group. Capital intensity is moderate at 3.4% of sales.

**Conservative Financial Policy.** Management has committed to maintain net leverage in a range of 2.0x to 2.5x over the cycle and has paused its repurchase program until it returns to that range. Aldermoor targets a dividend payout ratio of 35%.

**Adequate Liquidity.** At June 30, 2026, Aldermoor had $734 million of unrestricted cash and $2.0 billion available under its undrawn revolving credit facility maturing in 2030. Debt maturities are manageable: $400 million in fiscal 2027 and $650 million in fiscal 2028.

## Rating Sensitivities

**Factors that could, individually or collectively, lead to positive rating action/upgrade:**
- EBITDA leverage sustained below 2.0x
- FFO-adjusted leverage sustained below 2.5x
- Increased aftermarket and service mix above 35% of revenue, reducing cyclicality

**Factors that could, individually or collectively, lead to negative rating action/downgrade:**
- EBITDA leverage sustained above 3.0x, or failure to reduce it below 2.8x by the end of fiscal 2027
- FFO-adjusted leverage sustained above 3.5x
- A large debt-funded acquisition or resumption of aggressive shareholder returns before leverage declines
- A material and sustained decline in EBITDA margin below 15% (fiscal 2026: 17.3%)

## Credit Profile Summary

### Business Profile

Aldermoor ranks among the three largest providers of engineered fluid-handling equipment in North America and ranks fifth globally. The business benefits from a large installed base (estimated at 1.9 million units) and standards-driven specifications that favor incumbent suppliers. Aftermarket parts and service account for 27% of sales but 41% of gross profit, providing a stable earnings contribution. The weaker aspects of the business profile are cyclicality in capital project spending and exposure to commodity-linked end-markets.

### Management and Governance

Management is experienced and has a good record of meeting guidance. The CEO, Frances Oyelaran, joined in 2019 and was formerly head of the process automation division. The board is majority independent. Calloway views governance as neutral to the rating.

### Liquidity and Debt Structure

Aldermoor's liquidity is sufficient to cover maturities, working capital swings and capital expenditures through fiscal 2028 even in a downside scenario. The senior unsecured notes are not guaranteed by subsidiaries and rank pari passu with the revolving credit facility, which is unsecured. The revolver contains a single financial covenant of maximum consolidated leverage of 3.75x, with headroom of about 0.65x.

## Calloway's Base Case and Downside Scenario

**Base case:**
- Revenue growth of 6.0% in fiscal 2027 and 4.3% in fiscal 2028, reflecting Tessel's full-year contribution and mid-single-digit organic growth
- EBITDA margin expands to 18.1% in FY2027 from synergy realization of $65 million by FY2028
- Capital expenditures 3.4% of sales; dividends $245 million annually
- Debt repayment of $200 million in each of FY2027 and FY2028
- No acquisitions of consequence

**Downside case:** A 10% decline in process industry orders, stalling revenue growth and reducing EBITDA by 9% in FY2027. Leverage would peak at about 3.2x and recover to below 3.0x by FY2028. This is within the tolerance for the current rating.

## Rating Definitions and Scale

The 'BBB' category indicates that expectations of default risk are currently low. The capacity for payment of financial commitments is considered adequate but adverse business or economic conditions are more likely to impair this capacity. The modifiers "+" or "-" may be appended to a rating to denote relative status within major rating categories. An Outlook indicates the direction a rating is likely to move over a one- to two-year period. 'F2' indicates good intrinsic capacity for timely payment of financial commitments.
