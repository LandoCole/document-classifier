# RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on August 28, 2026, between Greenway Property Management LLC, as agent for the owner ("Landlord"), and Jordan K. Reyes and Casey L. Reyes ("Tenant").

## 1. PREMISES

Landlord leases to Tenant the apartment located at 418 Alder Street, Unit 3B, Portland, Oregon 97214 (the "Premises"), including one assigned parking space (No. 14) and storage locker 3B. The Premises shall be used only as a private residence for the Tenant and the following occupants: Jordan K. Reyes, Casey L. Reyes, and one minor child.

## 2. TERM

The term of this Lease begins on October 1, 2026 and ends at 11:59 p.m. on September 30, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end of the term without a new lease, the tenancy shall become month to month under the terms of this Lease, and either party may terminate with the notice required by Oregon law.

## 3. RENT

Tenant agrees to pay rent of $2,145.00 per month, due on the first day of each month, in advance. Rent may be paid online through the resident portal, by check payable to Greenway Property Management LLC, or by electronic transfer. If rent is not received by the fifth day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $50.00, and an additional $10.00 for each day thereafter, up to a maximum of $150.00 in a month, as permitted by law. A fee of $35.00 will be charged for any payment returned by the bank.

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,145.00. The deposit will be held in a trust account and returned, less lawful deductions for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear, within 31 days after Tenant vacates and returns all keys. Tenant may not apply the deposit to the last month's rent.

## 5. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

Landlord pays for water, sewer and garbage. Tenant pays for electricity, internet, cable and any other utilities. Tenant shall place electricity service in Tenant's name by the start of the term.

## 6. PETS

No pets are allowed without the prior written consent of Landlord. Tenant has requested permission to keep one cat; an addendum with a pet deposit of $300 and monthly pet rent of $25 is attached.

## 7. MAINTENANCE AND ALTERATIONS

Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and sanitary, shall promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs and shall not make alterations, paint or install fixtures without written consent. Landlord shall maintain the Premises in habitable condition and make timely repairs of the plumbing, heating, electrical and appliances provided. Emergency maintenance requests may be made at (503) 555-0188.

## 8. ENTRY

Landlord may enter the Premises with at least 24 hours' written notice for inspection, repairs or showing to prospective tenants, and without notice in an emergency.

## 9. SMOKING

Smoking and vaping of any substance is prohibited inside the Premises and within 25 feet of any entrance or window.

## 10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent.

## 11. DEFAULT

If Tenant fails to pay rent or breaches any other term of this Lease, Landlord may terminate the tenancy and pursue all remedies available under Oregon law after giving the notice required by law.

## 12. INSURANCE

Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's personal property. Tenant is required to maintain renter's insurance with at least $100,000 in liability coverage and to provide proof before move-in.

## 13. LEAD-BASED PAINT DISCLOSURE

Housing built before 1978 may contain lead-based paint. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the EPA pamphlet "Protect Your Family From Lead in Your Home." The Premises was built in 1962.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW

This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon. This is the entire agreement of the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both.
