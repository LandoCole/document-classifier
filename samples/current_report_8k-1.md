## Brantley Foods Reports Third Quarter Fiscal 2025 Results; Raises Full-Year Outlook

COLUMBUS, Ohio, August 5, 2025 -- Brantley Foods Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: BRFH) today reported results for its fiscal third quarter ended June 28, 2025.

Third quarter highlights:
- Net sales of $1.46 billion, up 4.1% from the prior-year quarter
- Diluted earnings per share of $0.88, compared with $0.71 a year ago
- Adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.94, up 14.6%
- Company raises fiscal 2025 adjusted EPS outlook to a range of $3.55 to $3.65

"Our branded snacks and frozen meals businesses both delivered volume growth this quarter, and productivity programs lifted gross margin by 120 basis points," said Raymond T. Castellanos, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. "We are confident enough in the second half to raise our outlook."

| (in millions, except per share) | Q3 FY2025 | Q3 FY2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net sales | $1,462.8 | $1,405.2 | 4.1% |
| Gross profit | 431.5 | 400.7 | 7.7% |
| Operating income | 168.3 | 147.9 | 13.8% |
| Net income | 109.6 | 88.7 | 23.6% |
| Diluted EPS | $0.88 | $0.71 | 23.9% |

The company will discuss these results on a conference call today at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time.
