## Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Operating revenue for the second quarter of 2025 increased 5.2% to $418.2 million. Truckload revenue per tractor per week rose 3.8% while average tractor count was 6,240, down 1.1%. Our operating ratio improved to 91.8% from 92.9% in the second quarter of 2024. Fuel and operating supplies expense declined as a percentage of revenue due to lower diesel prices.

Liquidity. Net cash provided by operating activities for the six months ended June 30, 2025 was $118.2 million. We spent $96.4 million on net capital expenditures. We believe available cash, cash flow from operations and availability of $160.0 million under our revolving credit facility will be adequate for at least the next twelve months.
