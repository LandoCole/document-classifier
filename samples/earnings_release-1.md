# Lumenfield Retail Group Reports Third Quarter Fiscal 2025 Results

**Comparable sales increase 3.6%; diluted EPS of $1.12; company updates full-year guidance**

PORTLAND, Ore., November 20, 2025 -- Lumenfield Retail Group, Inc. (NYSE: LMFD) today announced financial results for the thirteen weeks ended November 1, 2025 ("third quarter fiscal 2025").

## Third Quarter Fiscal 2025 Highlights
- Net sales increased 5.8% to $2.31 billion, compared with $2.18 billion in the third quarter of fiscal 2024.
- Comparable sales increased 3.6%, driven by a 2.9% increase in transactions and a 0.7% increase in average ticket.
- Digital sales grew 11.4% and represented 27.5% of total sales.
- Operating income was $186.4 million, or 8.1% of net sales, compared with $161.7 million, or 7.4%, last year.
- Diluted earnings per share were $1.12, compared with $0.94 in the prior-year period. Adjusted diluted earnings per share, a non-GAAP measure, were $1.19.
- The company opened 11 new stores and closed 3, ending the quarter with 1,042 stores.

"Our teams delivered a strong quarter in a choppy consumer environment," said Patricia L. Hendricks, Chief Executive Officer of Lumenfield. "Customers responded to our new private brand assortment, and our supply chain investments are allowing us to get product to stores faster and at lower cost. We enter the holiday season with a clean inventory position and a clear plan."

"Gross margin expanded 90 basis points to 36.2%, reflecting lower markdowns and freight costs," said David K. Ostrowski, Chief Financial Officer. "We repurchased 1.4 million shares for $92 million in the quarter and ended the period with $418 million in cash and no borrowings under our revolving credit facility."

## Fiscal 2025 Outlook
The company now expects:
- Net sales growth of 5.0% to 5.5% (previously 4.5% to 5.5%)
- Comparable sales growth of 3.0% to 3.5%
- Adjusted diluted EPS of $5.38 to $5.46 (previously $5.25 to $5.45)
- Capital expenditures of approximately $410 million
- An effective tax rate of approximately 24.5%

The company's outlook excludes the impact of any future share repurchases beyond those completed to date and any unusual items.
