# KESTREL FREIGHT SYSTEMS, INC.

## $500,000,000 5.125% Senior Notes due 2034

We are offering $500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of our 5.125% Senior Notes due 2034 (the "notes"). The notes will mature on November 15, 2034. Interest on the notes will accrue from November 4, 2026 and will be payable semiannually in arrears on May 15 and November 15 of each year, beginning May 15, 2027.

We may redeem some or all of the notes at any time before August 15, 2034 (three months before maturity) by paying a "make-whole" premium. On or after that date we may redeem the notes at par. If we experience a change of control triggering event, we must offer to repurchase the notes at 101% of their principal amount plus accrued interest.

The notes will be our senior unsecured obligations and will rank equally in right of payment with all of our existing and future senior unsecured indebtedness, and will be effectively subordinated to our secured indebtedness to the extent of the value of the collateral. The notes will be guaranteed on a senior unsecured basis by certain of our domestic subsidiaries that guarantee our revolving credit facility.

Investing in the notes involves risks. See "Risk Factors" beginning on page 18.

Price: 99.482% plus accrued interest, if any, from November 4, 2026

The notes have not been, and will not be, registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the "Securities Act"), or the securities laws of any other jurisdiction. The notes are being offered only to persons reasonably believed to be qualified institutional buyers in reliance on Rule 144A under the Securities Act and to non-U.S. persons outside the United States in reliance on Regulation S. Prospective purchasers are hereby notified that sellers may be relying on the exemption from Section 5 of the Securities Act provided by Rule 144A.

## SUMMARY

### Our Company

Kestrel Freight Systems, Inc. is a leading North American provider of less-than-truckload (LTL) freight transportation and freight brokerage services. We operate 212 terminals and 31 distribution centers and employed approximately 11,400 people as of September 30, 2026. For the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, we generated revenue of $3,421.6 million, net income of $231.4 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $612.7 million.

### Recent Developments

On October 22, 2026, we expect to announce preliminary third quarter results: revenue of approximately $886 million and net income of approximately $63 million. These preliminary figures are subject to completion of our quarter-end close procedures and may change.

### The Offering

Use of proceeds. We estimate that the net proceeds from this offering will be approximately $493.6 million after deducting the initial purchasers' discount and estimated offering expenses. We intend to use the net proceeds to repay $350.0 million outstanding under our Term Loan A, to redeem our 3.90% Senior Notes due 2027 ($125.0 million) and the remainder for general corporate purposes.

Covenants. The indenture governing the notes will, among other things, restrict our ability and the ability of our restricted subsidiaries to incur liens, enter into sale and leaseback transactions, and merge or consolidate. These covenants are subject to important exceptions.

Events of default include failure to pay principal or interest, breach of covenants, cross-acceleration on indebtedness exceeding $75 million, and certain bankruptcy events.

## RISK FACTORS (EXCERPT)

Risks Relating to the Notes: Our substantial indebtedness could adversely affect our financial health. The notes are unsecured and effectively subordinated to our secured debt. Holders may be unable to resell the notes because there is no established market and the notes are subject to transfer restrictions. We may not be able to repurchase the notes upon a change of control. Ratings may be lowered or withdrawn.

Risks Relating to Our Business: Our business is cyclical and sensitive to fuel prices, labor costs, driver availability, and the general U.S. economy. Network consolidation may not yield the savings we expect.
