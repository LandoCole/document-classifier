FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

PINECREST SAVINGS BANCORP, INC. ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2025 EARNINGS

Net income of $11.8 million for the quarter; record annual earnings of $43.6 million; Board declares quarterly dividend of $0.24 per share

ASHEVILLE, N.C. -- (January 22, 2026) -- Pinecrest Savings Bancorp, Inc. (Nasdaq: PCSB) ("Pinecrest" or the "Company"), the holding company for Pinecrest Savings Bank, today reported net income of $11.8 million, or $0.92 per diluted share, for the quarter ended December 31, 2025, compared with $9.6 million, or $0.74 per diluted share, for the quarter ended December 31, 2024. For the full year, net income was $43.6 million, or $3.39 per diluted share, compared with $37.1 million, or $2.86 per diluted share, in 2024.

"2025 was a year of disciplined growth for Pinecrest," said Gerald W. Thackeray, President and Chief Executive Officer. "Loans grew 7.9%, core deposits grew 5.2%, and our net interest margin expanded for the fourth consecutive quarter. Credit quality remained excellent, with nonperforming assets at just 0.31% of total assets."

### Fourth Quarter Highlights
- Net interest income was $38.9 million, an increase of 9.1% from the fourth quarter of 2024. Net interest margin was 3.61% compared with 3.38% a year ago and 3.55% in the third quarter of 2025.
- The provision for credit losses was $1.6 million compared with $2.1 million in the prior-year quarter. The allowance for credit losses was 1.19% of total loans.
- Noninterest income was $8.7 million, up 6.3%, led by wealth management fees and mortgage banking income.
- Noninterest expense was $29.4 million, up 3.8%. The efficiency ratio was 61.7%.
- Return on average assets was 1.12% and return on average tangible common equity was 13.9%.

### Balance Sheet
Total assets were $4.26 billion at December 31, 2025, compared with $3.98 billion a year earlier. Total loans were $3.07 billion, up $225.4 million, or 7.9%, from December 31, 2024. Total deposits were $3.58 billion, up 5.6%. Tangible book value per share increased to $26.84 from $24.31. The Company's common equity Tier 1 capital ratio was 12.4% and total risk-based capital ratio was 14.5%.
