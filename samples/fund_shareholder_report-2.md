## Management's Discussion of Fund Performance

### What factors influenced performance?
The Fund returned 11.6% (without sales charge) for the twelve months ended June 30, 2025. Its blended benchmark of 60% broad U.S. equity and 40% U.S. aggregate bond returned 12.3%. The equity portion of the portfolio benefited from holdings in communication services and financials and was hampered by an underweight to the largest technology names. Within fixed income, security selection in investment-grade corporate bonds was a positive contributor, while the portfolio's slightly shorter duration modestly detracted as yields fell late in the period.
