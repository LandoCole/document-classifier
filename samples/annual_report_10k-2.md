PART I

ITEM 1. BUSINESS

General. Cedar Hollow Bancorp, Inc. (the "Company") is a Pennsylvania bank holding company registered under the Bank Holding Company Act of 1956. It owns all of the outstanding stock of Cedar Hollow Savings Bank (the "Bank"), a Pennsylvania-chartered savings bank founded in 1893. The Bank conducts business through seven full-service offices in Union, Snyder and Northumberland Counties, Pennsylvania. At December 31, 2024 the Company had total assets of $641.8 million, total loans of $437.2 million, total deposits of $548.6 million and shareholders' equity of $61.3 million.

Lending activities. The Bank originates one-to-four family residential mortgages, commercial real estate loans, agricultural loans, and consumer loans. Residential mortgage loans were 41% of the loan portfolio, commercial real estate 33%, commercial and agricultural 17% and consumer 9%. The allowance for credit losses was $5.6 million, or 1.28% of total loans, at year end. Nonperforming loans were $2.1 million, or 0.48% of total loans.

Deposits and other sources of funds. Deposits are the Bank's primary source of funds. At year end, noninterest-bearing demand deposits were $104.2 million, interest-bearing demand and money market $231.7 million, savings $92.4 million and time deposits $120.3 million. The Bank had no brokered deposits. It also had $34.0 million of advances from a Federal Home Loan Bank.

Competition. The Bank competes with regional banks, credit unions and online lenders. Union County has a deposit market share to the Bank's offices of approximately 22%.

Supervision and regulation. The Company is subject to supervision by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. The Bank is subject to regulation by the Pennsylvania Department of Banking and Securities and the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation. At December 31, 2024 the Bank's Tier 1 leverage ratio was 9.8% and its total risk-based capital ratio was 15.1%, both above the levels required to be considered "well capitalized."

Employees. The Company had 112 full-time equivalent employees at December 31, 2024.

Available information. The Company's Annual Report on Form 10-K, Quarterly Reports on Form 10-Q and Current Reports on Form 8-K are available on its website at www.cedarhollowbank.example.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Net income for 2024 was $5,218,000, or $1.79 per share, compared with $4,861,000, or $1.66 per share, for 2023. Net interest income increased 4.3% to $19.6 million, and the net interest margin was 3.28% compared with 3.19% in 2023. The provision for credit losses was $420,000. Noninterest income was $4.1 million and noninterest expense was $17.3 million. The efficiency ratio was 72.9%.
