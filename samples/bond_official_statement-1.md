The City of Riverbend, Ohio (the "City") is issuing its General Obligation Bonds, Series 2026 (Unlimited Tax) (the "Series 2026 Bonds") as fully registered bonds, in denominations of $5,000 or any integral multiple thereof. The Series 2026 Bonds will be issued in book-entry only form through The Depository Trust Company, New York, New York ("DTC"). Purchasers will not receive certificates representing their interest in the Series 2026 Bonds.

Interest on the Series 2026 Bonds is payable on June 1 and December 1 of each year, commencing June 1, 2027. Principal and interest will be paid to DTC, which will remit payment to its participants.

The Series 2026 Bonds are being issued to (i) finance the costs of street reconstruction, a replacement fire station and improvements to the City's water treatment plant (the "Project"), and (ii) pay costs of issuance.

The Series 2026 Bonds are general obligations of the City. The full faith and credit of the City are pledged to the payment of principal of and interest on the Series 2026 Bonds. The City has covenanted to levy and collect ad valorem taxes on all taxable property in the City, unlimited as to rate and amount, in amounts sufficient to pay debt service when due.

## INTRODUCTORY STATEMENT

This Official Statement, which includes the cover page, inside cover and appendices, provides information about the City of Riverbend, Ohio (the "City") and its $38,500,000 General Obligation Bonds, Series 2026 (Unlimited Tax) (the "Series 2026 Bonds"). The Series 2026 Bonds are being issued under Chapter 133 of the Ohio Revised Code, the Constitution of the State of Ohio, and Ordinance No. 2026-68 passed by the City Council on September 22, 2026 (the "Bond Legislation"). The Series 2026 Bonds were authorized by the electors of the City at an election held on November 5, 2024, at which 63.4% of voters approved Issue 7, authorizing up to $45,000,000 of general obligation bonds for the Project.

The descriptions and summaries of documents in this Official Statement do not purport to be comprehensive or definitive, and all references are qualified in their entirety by the complete documents. Capitalized terms not defined have the meanings given in the Bond Legislation.

## THE SERIES 2026 BONDS

### General

The Series 2026 Bonds will be dated their date of delivery and will bear interest from that date at the rates per annum set forth on the inside cover, payable semiannually on June 1 and December 1, beginning June 1, 2027. Interest is computed on the basis of a 360-day year of twelve 30-day months. The Series 2026 Bonds mature on December 1 in the years and amounts shown on the cover.

### Book-Entry Only System

DTC will act as securities depository for the Series 2026 Bonds. The Series 2026 Bonds will be issued as fully registered securities registered in the name of Cede & Co. One fully registered bond certificate will be issued for each maturity, in the aggregate principal amount of such maturity, and will be deposited with DTC. Purchases of beneficial interests will be made in book-entry form in denominations of $5,000 or integral multiples thereof. The City, the Paying Agent and the Underwriters have no responsibility for the records of DTC or its participants.

### Redemption

Optional redemption. The Series 2026 Bonds maturing on and after December 1, 2035 are subject to optional redemption, in whole or in part, in any order of maturity selected by the City, on any date on or after December 1, 2034, at 100% of the principal amount plus accrued interest.

Notice of redemption. Notice will be mailed by the Paying Agent not less than 30 nor more than 60 days before the redemption date to the registered owners. If any bond is called for redemption and funds are on deposit, interest will cease to accrue on the redemption date.

## SECURITY AND SOURCES OF PAYMENT

The Series 2026 Bonds are general obligations of the City, and the full faith, credit and revenue of the City are pledged for payment of the principal and interest thereon. The City has covenanted in the Bond Legislation to levy ad valorem taxes on all property in the City subject to ad valorem taxation by the City, without limitation as to rate or amount, in amounts sufficient to pay debt service on the Series 2026 Bonds as it comes due. The proceeds of this levy will be deposited in the Bond Retirement Fund, a segregated fund held by the City Finance Director, and used solely for debt service.

The Series 2026 Bonds are not payable from any particular fund of the City other than the Bond Retirement Fund, but if that fund is insufficient, the City must pay from any other funds lawfully available. Under Ohio law, the general obligations of the City have priority in payment over other uses of ad valorem tax revenue of the City. There is no debt service reserve fund for the Series 2026 Bonds.

The City has never defaulted on the payment of principal or interest of any of its bonds or notes.

## THE CITY

The City of Riverbend is located in Summit County, Ohio, approximately 12 miles south of Akron. The City covers 31.4 square miles and had a 2020 Census population of 78,246. It operates under a charter adopted in 1962 which provides for a Mayor-Council form of government. The Mayor is elected to a four-year term and the seven members of Council serve two-year terms. The City provides police and fire protection, street maintenance, parks and recreation, water and sewer service, and general administrative services. The City has 612 full-time employees, of whom 74% are represented by collective bargaining agreements, which expire between December 31, 2027 and December 31, 2029. There have been no work stoppages in the past 25 years.

The City's economy is anchored by advanced manufacturing, health care and logistics. The ten largest employers in the City are shown below.

### Income Tax

The City levies a municipal income tax of 2.25% on wages and net profits, of which 0.50% is dedicated to capital improvements and debt service. Under Ohio law, any increase in the rate requires voter approval. Income tax collections represent 65.1% of general fund revenues in 2025. The top ten taxpayers (withholding) account for 21% of income tax receipts, and the largest employer, Aldermoor Industrial Corp., accounts for 8%.

## PENSION AND OTHER POST-EMPLOYMENT BENEFITS

City employees participate in the Ohio Public Employees Retirement System and police and fire employees in the Ohio Police and Fire Pension Fund. ## RISK FACTORS

Prospective purchasers should consider, among other things, the following risks. Limitations on remedies. Remedies of bondholders are limited by bankruptcy, insolvency and other laws affecting creditors' rights and by the exercise of judicial discretion. Concentration of income tax receipts. A significant decline in operations of one of the City's largest employers would reduce income tax revenue. Secondary market. There may be no secondary market for the Series 2026 Bonds, and prices may be volatile. Changes in tax law. Legislation could affect the tax-exempt status of interest. Cybersecurity. The City relies on information systems and has experienced no material breach to date. State aid. The State of Ohio could reduce local government fund distributions. Property values. A decline in property values could affect the property tax levy.

## TAX MATTERS

Bond Counsel's opinion is described on the cover. The Code imposes requirements that must be met after the delivery of the Series 2026 Bonds in order for interest to be excluded from gross income. The City has covenanted to comply with these requirements. Failure to comply could cause interest to be included in gross income retroactively to the date of issuance. The Series 2026 Bonds maturing in 2027 through 2034 are being sold at a premium, which is amortized for tax purposes and reduces the owner's basis in the bond. Owners should consult their own tax advisers about premium amortization, collateral federal tax consequences and state and local taxes.
