# ASPENWOOD GLOBAL DIVIDEND GROWTH ETF
## NYSE Arca: AGDG

Data as of 09/30/2025 | Quarterly fact sheet

### Why invest
AGDG seeks long-term growth of capital and rising income by owning 60 to 80 companies around the world with histories of increasing their dividends and the financial strength to continue doing so. The fund is actively managed and looks for businesses with returns on invested capital above 12% and payout ratios below 65%.

### Manager commentary
Global equities advanced in the third quarter as easing inflation data and resilient earnings supported risk assets. The fund returned 5.8% for the quarter, trailing its benchmark by 0.4 percentage points. An overweight to industrials contributed, as did stock selection in health care. An underweight to information technology and an allocation to consumer staples detracted. During the quarter we added a European medical device maker and a Japanese industrial automation company, and we exited a U.S. utility after a strong run. We continue to emphasize quality companies with pricing power. Portfolio managers: Camille R. Odegaard, CFA; Hiroshi Tanabe.
