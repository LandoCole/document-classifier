**Q3 Preview: Seat Growth Slowing, Margin Guide a Coin Flip. Trimming PT.**

We reduce our price target to $286 from $310 and keep our Hold rating ahead of Marlow's Q3 report on October 29. Our checks suggest net new seats slowed for a second straight quarter, and while the stock is no longer expensive at 6.8x EV/2027E sales, we do not see a catalyst to move us to Buy.

**What we expect for Q3 (quarter ending 9/30)**

| | Our Est. | Consensus | Q3 2025A | Y/Y |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue ($m) | 1,214 | 1,221 | 1,038 | +17.0% |
| Subscription revenue ($m) | 1,102 | 1,108 | 933 | +18.1% |
| Non-GAAP op. margin | 31.2% | 31.8% | 30.4% | +80 bps |
| Non-GAAP EPS ($) | 2.21 | 2.26 | 1.84 | +20.1% |
| Net new ARR ($m) | 148 | 155 | 144 | +2.8% |

We are below the Street on revenue by 0.6% and on EPS by 2.2%. We estimate FY2026 revenue of $4,712 million (+16.5%) and non-GAAP EPS of $8.71. For FY2027 we are at $5,418 million and $10.04.

**Three points**

1. *Seat compression.* Our survey of 62 enterprise IT buyers found 34% planning to reduce Marlow seats at renewal, up from 27% in our June survey. The reduction is offset by price uplift on the Platform tier, but we think pricing power is peaking.
2. *AI bundle.* Attach for the new assistant add-on is tracking to roughly 9% of the base versus management's 15% year-end ambition. We model 11% by Q4.
3. *Margin.* Management guided to a full-year non-GAAP operating margin of 30.5% to 31.5%. We are at 31.0%. Hiring has been heavier than expected, so we see risk to the low end.

**Valuation**

Our $286 target is based on 28x our 2027E non-GAAP EPS of $10.04 less a $1 per share adjustment for stock-based compensation dilution, or about 6.9x EV/2027E sales. Marlow trades at 27.8x. The target implies 2.4% upside to the current price.

**Credit note:** Marlow's 3.75% convertible notes due 2029 are rated BBB+ by Calloway Credit Analytics, whose recent report affirmed the rating. Credit is not a concern; we flag it only because the convert is in the money above $270 and would add about 4.1 million diluted shares if converted.

**Risks:** Upside risk includes faster AI attach, a buyback upsizing, and a stronger federal vertical. Downside risks include seat reductions, FX, and a slowdown in public sector budgets.
