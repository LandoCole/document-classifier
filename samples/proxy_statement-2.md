# PROPOSED ACQUISITION -- YOUR VOTE IS VERY IMPORTANT

To the Stockholders of Lakemont Software, Inc.:

On behalf of the Board of Directors, I invite you to attend a special meeting of stockholders of Lakemont Software, Inc. ("Lakemont") to be held on December 9, 2025 at 9:00 a.m., Pacific Time, at the offices of Lakemont at 55 Harbor Way, Seattle, Washington 98104.

On September 22, 2025, Lakemont entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Tidewell Holdings, Inc. ("Tidewell") and its wholly owned subsidiary, Tidewell Merger Sub, Inc. Under the merger agreement, Merger Sub will merge with and into Lakemont, and Lakemont will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Tidewell. If the merger is completed, each share of Lakemont common stock will be converted into the right to receive $27.50 in cash, without interest. This represents a premium of 38% to the closing price of Lakemont common stock on September 19, 2025, the last trading day before public announcement.

The Lakemont Board unanimously determined that the merger is advisable and in the best interests of Lakemont and its stockholders and unanimously recommends that you vote FOR the proposal to adopt the merger agreement.

Because Tidewell is paying all-cash consideration, this document is a proxy statement only; it is not a prospectus, and no Tidewell securities are being offered. Tidewell has not filed a registration statement on Form S-4 in connection with the merger.

## NOTICE OF SPECIAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS TO BE HELD ON DECEMBER 9, 2025

Notice is hereby given that a special meeting of stockholders of Lakemont Software, Inc. will be held for the following purposes:

1. To consider and vote on a proposal to adopt the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated September 22, 2025;
2. To consider and vote, on a non-binding advisory basis, on a proposal to approve the compensation that may become payable to Lakemont's named executive officers in connection with the merger; and
3. To approve an adjournment of the special meeting, if necessary, to solicit additional proxies.

Only holders of record at the close of business on October 28, 2025 are entitled to vote.

## QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

**Q: What is the proposed transaction?**
A: Tidewell will acquire Lakemont by merging a subsidiary into Lakemont. You will receive $27.50 in cash for each share of Lakemont common stock you own.

**Q: What vote is required to adopt the merger agreement?**
A: Adoption requires the affirmative vote of the holders of a majority of the outstanding shares of Lakemont common stock entitled to vote. As of the record date there were 22,640,118 shares outstanding. Your failure to vote has the same effect as a vote AGAINST adoption.

**Q: What happens if the merger is not completed?**
A: Lakemont would remain an independent public company and its common stock would continue to be listed on Nasdaq. Under specified circumstances, Lakemont may be required to pay Tidewell a termination fee of $41.2 million, or Tidewell may be required to pay Lakemont a reverse termination fee of $82.4 million.

**Q: Do I have appraisal rights?**
A: Yes. Holders who do not vote in favor of the merger and who properly demand appraisal may be entitled to seek a judicial determination of the fair value of their shares under Section 262 of the Delaware General Corporation Law.

**Q: When is the merger expected to close?**
A: We expect the merger to close in the first quarter of 2026, subject to stockholder approval and expiration of the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period.

## BACKGROUND OF THE MERGER

In June 2025, the Lakemont Board authorized management to explore strategic alternatives. Over the following ten weeks, representatives of Cartwright Lane Securities, Lakemont's financial advisor, contacted 14 potential buyers. Seven executed confidentiality agreements and three submitted preliminary proposals. On September 12, Tidewell submitted a final proposal at $27.50 per share. The Board met on September 21 and received an opinion from Cartwright Lane that, as of that date, the merger consideration was fair, from a financial point of view, to holders of Lakemont common stock.

## INTERESTS OF LAKEMONT'S DIRECTORS AND EXECUTIVE OFFICERS IN THE MERGER

Lakemont's executive officers have interests in the merger that are different from, or in addition to, yours, including accelerated vesting of equity awards. The estimated aggregate value of the payments to Lakemont's named executive officers is $18.7 million.
