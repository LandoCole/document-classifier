## LETTER TO SHAREHOLDERS

Dear Shareholder:

We are pleased to present the semi-annual report for the Redstone Municipal Income Fund (the "Fund") for the six months ended April 30, 2025. Municipal bonds posted modest gains over the period as investors weighed uncertainty about the path of interest rates against strong demand for tax-exempt income. The Fund's Class A shares returned 1.94% at net asset value, compared with a return of 1.62% for the Fund's benchmark, the Redstone National Investment Grade Municipal Index.

We thank you for your continued confidence in the Fund.

Sincerely,
Walter H. Pennington
President, Redstone Municipal Income Fund

## PERFORMANCE DISCUSSION

Municipal yields rose across the curve in the fourth quarter of 2024 and fell back in the first four months of 2025. Longer-maturity bonds underperformed shorter maturities as the yield curve steepened. Issuance in the municipal market totaled approximately $187 billion in the first four months of the year, 11% above the same period last year, with healthy reinvestment demand from maturing bonds and calls.

Over the period, the Fund benefited from an overweight to revenue bonds backed by essential services, including water and sewer systems, and to airport revenue bonds. Holdings in tobacco settlement bonds also contributed. A modest underweight to general obligation bonds of the largest states detracted. The Fund held 3.1% of net assets in below investment grade securities at period end. The Fund's effective duration was 6.4 years compared with 6.7 years for the benchmark.

## NOTES TO FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (EXCERPT)

Note 1 - Organization. The Redstone Municipal Income Fund is a series of Redstone Investment Trust, an open-end management investment company registered under the Investment Company Act of 1940. The Fund's investment objective is to provide a high level of current income exempt from federal income tax.

Note 2 - Significant accounting policies. Securities are valued using prices provided by an independent pricing service. Interest income is accrued daily. Dividends from net investment income are declared daily and paid monthly.

Proxy voting. A description of the policies and procedures the Fund uses to vote proxies and information on how the Fund voted during the most recent 12-month period ended June 30 are available without charge at www.redstone-funds.example or on the SEC's website at www.sec.gov.
