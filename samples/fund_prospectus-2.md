PRINCIPAL INVESTMENT STRATEGIES

The Fund is an exchange-traded fund that pursues its objective by investing, under normal circumstances, at least 80% of its net assets in U.S. Treasury securities with remaining maturities of three years or less. The Fund seeks to maintain a dollar-weighted average effective duration of between one and two years. The Fund is actively managed. The Adviser selects securities based on its view of the yield curve, relative value among Treasury notes, bills and inflation-protected securities, and liquidity. The Fund may also hold U.S. Treasury floating-rate notes and cash equivalents, and may engage in repurchase agreements collateralized by Treasury securities.

The Fund will not be diversified in the sense that it may invest a significant portion of its assets in securities of the U.S. government, which is not subject to diversification limits.

PRINCIPAL RISKS

As with all investments, you may lose money by investing in the Fund. The Fund's shares are not guaranteed or insured by the U.S. government.

- Interest Rate Risk. Fixed income securities fall in value when interest rates rise. Securities with longer durations are more sensitive.
- Credit and Government Obligations Risk. Although U.S. Treasury securities are backed by the full faith and credit of the U.S. government, a downgrade or default could reduce their value.
- Inflation Risk. Inflation may reduce the real value of the Fund's income and principal.
- ETF Structure Risks. Authorized Participants: Only authorized participants may engage in creation or redemption transactions directly with the Fund, and no other authorized participant may step forward if existing ones exit the business. Trading Issues: Shares may trade at a premium or discount to net asset value, and trading may be halted. Cash Redemption Risk: Because the Fund may redeem Creation Units in cash, it may realize capital gains.
- New Fund Risk. The Fund is new and has no operating history; there can be no assurance that it will grow to or maintain an economically viable size.

PURCHASE AND SALE OF FUND SHARES

The Fund will issue and redeem Shares at net asset value ("NAV") only in large blocks of 25,000 Shares (each block of Shares called a "Creation Unit"). Creation Units are issued and redeemed principally in-kind for securities included in the Fund's portfolio and, in certain circumstances, for cash. Individual Shares may only be purchased and sold on NYSE Arca through a broker-dealer. You can purchase and sell individual Shares of the Fund throughout the trading day like any publicly traded security. The price of Shares is based on market price, and because ETF shares trade at market prices rather than NAV, Shares may trade at a price greater than NAV (premium) or less than NAV (discount). Information on the Fund's NAV, market price, premiums and discounts and bid-ask spreads will be available at www.northgate-etf.example.

TAX INFORMATION

The Fund's distributions will generally be taxed as ordinary income or capital gains, although interest on U.S. Treasury obligations may be exempt from state and local income taxes. Distributions from the Fund are expected to be paid monthly.
