## Kestrel at a Glance

- Founded 1994; headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee
- 11,400 employees across 212 terminals and 31 distribution centers
- Asset-light brokerage plus asset-based less-than-truckload (LTL) network
- Trailing twelve-month revenue: $3.42 billion
- Top 100 customers average tenure: 14 years
- Investment-grade balance sheet; net leverage 1.6x

## Our Strategy: Three Priorities

1. Densify the network
   - Consolidate 19 overlapping terminals into 11 by mid-2027
2. Grow higher-margin brokerage
   - Digital freight marketplace now 38% of brokerage loads
3. Return cash
   - $250 million repurchase authorization; dividend raised 8% in Q2

## Market Opportunity

- U.S. freight market: ~$1.0 trillion annually
- LTL is ~$90 billion and consolidating; top 5 carriers hold ~58% share
- Shippers shifting to fewer, larger carrier relationships
- Nearshoring driving volume growth in the Southeast and Texas corridors
- Kestrel share of addressable LTL market: ~2.9%

## Network Consolidation Progress

- 6 of 19 terminals closed; 5 more scheduled for Q1 2027
- Door utilization up from 71% to 78% in consolidated markets
- Run-rate savings achieved: $17 million; target $48 million by end of 2027
- One-time costs of $22 million recorded year to date, in line with plan
