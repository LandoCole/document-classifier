### Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis (IPF)

- ~200,000 patients in the U.S.; ~5,000 new diagnoses per month
- Median survival 3 to 5 years from diagnosis
- Two approved therapies slow decline but do not stop it; ~40% discontinue within a year due to tolerability
- Estimated U.S. market > $4 billion annually

---

### HB-204 Mechanism

- Selective inhibitor of the LOXL2 pathway
- Reduces collagen crosslinking in preclinical models
- Once-daily oral tablet, no liver monitoring required
- Composition-of-matter patent expires 2041, before any extensions

---

### Phase 2 Results (BREEZE-0)

- 186 patients, 24 weeks, randomized 1:1:1
- Primary endpoint: change in forced vital capacity (FVC)

| Arm | n | Mean FVC change (mL) |
|---|---|---|
| Placebo | 62 | -118 |
| HB-204 100 mg | 61 | -52 |
| HB-204 200 mg | 63 | -31 |

- Discontinuation due to adverse events: 6.3% (200 mg) vs 4.8% (placebo)
- Most common AEs: nausea, headache, nasopharyngitis
