SABLEWOOD HEALTH PARTNERS, INC. (NYSE: SWHP)
Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call
February 26, 2026 -- 8:30 AM ET

CORPORATE PARTICIPANTS
Hollis Granger -- Director, Investor Relations
Dr. Miriam T. Alvarado -- Chief Executive Officer
Peter J. Vandersloot -- Chief Financial Officer

PRESENTATION

Operator: Good morning, ladies and gentlemen, and welcome to the Sablewood Health Partners fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings call. At this time all lines are in a listen-only mode. Following the presentation we will conduct a question-and-answer session. If anyone should require operator assistance during the conference, please press star zero on your touch-tone telephone. This call is being recorded. I will now hand the conference over to Hollis Granger. Please go ahead.

Hollis Granger: Thank you, and good morning. Before we begin, I'd like to remind you that today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which involve risks that are described in our SEC filings. Our press release was issued last night and is on our website; we expect to file our Form 10-K next week. Miriam?

Miriam Alvarado: Thanks, Hollis. Good morning, everyone. 2025 was a year in which we did what we said we would do. We served 4.2 million members at year end, up 9%, and generated full-year revenue of $11.8 billion, up 11%. Adjusted earnings per share for the year were $7.92, at the upper end of our guidance.

The medical cost trend was the big story for our industry, and I want to be direct about it. Our medical loss ratio for the fourth quarter was 86.4%, about 60 basis points higher than we'd planned, driven mostly by outpatient surgical and specialty drug costs in our Medicare Advantage book. We've already taken pricing and benefit actions for 2026, and we're exiting 14 counties where we couldn't reach adequate margins.

Peter?

Peter Vandersloot: Thank you, Miriam. Fourth quarter revenue was $3.05 billion. Adjusted earnings per share were $1.61 versus $1.74 in the prior-year quarter. For the full year, our medical loss ratio was 85.2%, compared with 84.7% in 2024, and our SG&A ratio improved 40 basis points to 9.8%. We generated operating cash flow of $842 million and returned $450 million to shareholders through repurchases.

For 2026, we are guiding revenue of $12.9 billion to $13.2 billion, an adjusted medical loss ratio of 85.0% to 85.6%, and adjusted EPS of $8.30 to $8.60. We expect about 60% of earnings in the first half. With that, operator, let's open it up.

QUESTION-AND-ANSWER SESSION

Operator: Our first question comes from the line of Evan Torrance with Whitlock Dunne. Please proceed.

Evan Torrance -- Whitlock Dunne: Good morning. Thanks for the question. On the Medicare Advantage cost trend, how much of the 2026 pricing action was already in your bids when you submitted them last June versus incremental benefit changes?

Miriam Alvarado: Evan, the bulk of it was in the bids. We saw the utilization pattern emerging in the second quarter and priced for a mid-single-digit trend. The incremental pieces are supplemental benefit reductions that we announced in the fall.

Evan Torrance: And is that consistent with the margin recovery you've embedded in your guidance?

Peter Vandersloot: Yes. The guidance assumes about 40 basis points of MLR improvement in that segment, which is roughly two-thirds of the gap.

Operator: Our next question is from Nadia Qureshi with Hartley Brook Securities.

Nadia Qureshi -- Hartley Brook Securities: Thanks. Can you give us the quantum of the county exits, and what does it do to membership?

Peter Vandersloot: The 14 counties represent about 85,000 members, or 2% of our total. We've factored that into the 2026 revenue range.

Nadia Qureshi: Thank you.

Operator: There are no further questions at this time. I'd now like to turn the call back over to Dr. Alvarado for any closing remarks.

Miriam Alvarado: Thank you all. We appreciate your interest in Sablewood and look forward to speaking with you next quarter.

Operator: This concludes today's teleconference. You may disconnect your lines at this time.
