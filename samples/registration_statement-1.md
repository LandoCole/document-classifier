## PROSPECTUS SUMMARY

This summary highlights information contained elsewhere in this prospectus. It does not contain all of the information that you should consider before investing in our common stock. You should read this entire prospectus carefully, including "Risk Factors," "Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations" and our consolidated financial statements and the related notes.

### Our Company

Marrowbrook provides a cloud data platform that helps mid-sized manufacturers and distributors unify data from enterprise resource planning systems, warehouse scanners, machine sensors and supplier portals into a single governed model. Our customers use Marrowbrook to forecast demand, trace the provenance of components and automate compliance reporting. As of June 30, 2025, we served 1,184 customers in 31 countries, including 212 customers with annual recurring revenue ("ARR") above $100,000.

We were founded in 2015 by Jonathan Reyes and Dr. Priscilla Aranda after they experienced the cost of fragmented data first-hand at a regional auto parts distributor. Since then we have built a platform that can be deployed in under six weeks, compared with a typical nine to fourteen months for a custom data warehouse project.

### Our Market Opportunity

We estimate that the addressable market for operational data platforms among industrial companies with $50 million to $2 billion in annual revenue in North America and Europe was approximately $19.4 billion in 2024, based on the number of such companies and our average ARR per customer of $127,000, and we believe it will grow as sensor deployments increase. This estimate is based on our internal analysis and involves assumptions and limitations.

### Our Growth Strategy

- Expand within our customer base. Our dollar-based net retention rate was 124% as of June 30, 2025.
- Acquire new customers through our direct sales team, which grew from 41 to 78 quota-carrying representatives in the last 24 months.
- Extend the platform with predictive maintenance and carbon accounting modules.
- Pursue international expansion, particularly in Germany, Poland and Mexico.

### Summary Risks

Our business is subject to numerous risks, including: we have a history of net losses and may not achieve profitability; our growth rate may decline; our sales cycles are long and unpredictable; we depend on a small number of cloud infrastructure providers; and our founders will control a majority of the voting power of our stock.

## RISK FACTORS

An investment in our common stock involves a high degree of risk. You should carefully consider the risks described below, together with all of the other information in this prospectus, before making an investment decision.

### Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

**We have a history of losses and may not achieve or sustain profitability.** We incurred net losses of $28.1 million and $23.7 million for 2023 and 2024, respectively, and $9.0 million for the six months ended June 30, 2025. As of June 30, 2025 we had an accumulated deficit of $147.6 million. We expect our operating expenses to increase as we invest in sales, product development and public company compliance.

**Our recent rapid growth may not be indicative of our future growth.** Our revenue grew 35% from 2023 to 2024. We expect our revenue growth rate to decline as our revenue base grows.

**Our sales cycle with large customers is long and unpredictable.** Our sales cycle from initial contact to contract signing averages five months for customers with ARR above $100,000. Delays could cause fluctuations in our quarterly results.

**We rely on third-party cloud infrastructure.** Substantially all of our platform runs on infrastructure provided by two public cloud providers. Disruptions in their services could interrupt our customers' access to our platform.

**Security incidents could harm our reputation.** Our platform stores sensitive supply chain and pricing data. Any breach could result in contractual liability and loss of customers.

### Risks Related to Our Intellectual Property and Regulation

**We may be sued for infringing the intellectual property rights of others.** As of June 30, 2025 we held 14 issued patents and 22 pending applications. Third parties may assert claims against us or our customers.

**Evolving privacy regimes such as the GDPR increase our compliance costs.**

### Risks Related to this Offering and Ownership of Our Common Stock

**Our founders will control a majority of the voting power of our capital stock and may take actions that other stockholders do not support.** Our dual class structure will concentrate voting control with Mr. Reyes and Dr. Aranda, who will together hold approximately 71% of the voting power following this offering.

**An active trading market for our common stock may never develop.** The initial public offering price will be determined by negotiations between us and the underwriters and may not be indicative of the price at which our common stock will trade after this offering.

**Future sales of our common stock may cause our stock price to decline.** After this offering, 13,780,000 shares of Class B and 24,640,000 shares of Class A held by existing stockholders will be subject to lock-up agreements for 180 days.

We estimate that we will receive net proceeds from the sale of shares in this offering of approximately $136.9 million, or $157.7 million if the underwriters exercise their option in full, based on an assumed initial public offering price of $17.00 per share, the midpoint of the price range on the cover of this prospectus, after deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and estimated offering expenses of $5.4 million payable by us.

A $1.00 increase (decrease) in the assumed initial public offering price would increase (decrease) the net proceeds to us by approximately $8.4 million, assuming the number of shares offered by us remains the same.

We currently intend to use approximately $22.0 million of the net proceeds to repay all outstanding indebtedness under our term loan, which bears interest at SOFR plus 6.25% and matures in March 2027. We intend to use the remainder for working capital and general corporate purposes, including approximately $40 million to expand our sales organization and approximately $25 million for research and development. We may also use a portion of the proceeds to acquire complementary businesses, although we have no present commitments.

## DILUTION

If you invest in our common stock in this offering, your ownership interest will be diluted immediately to the extent of the difference between the initial public offering price per share of our common stock and the pro forma as adjusted net tangible book value per share of our common stock after this offering.

Our pro forma net tangible book value as of June 30, 2025 was approximately $18.3 million, or $0.48 per share of common stock. Pro forma net tangible book value per share represents the amount of our total tangible assets less total liabilities, divided by the number of shares of common stock outstanding, after giving effect to the conversion of our convertible preferred stock.

After giving effect to our sale of 9,000,000 shares in this offering at an assumed initial public offering price of $17.00 per share, and after deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and estimated offering expenses, our pro forma as adjusted net tangible book value as of June 30, 2025 would have been approximately $155.2 million, or $3.27 per share. This represents an immediate increase in pro forma net tangible book value of $2.79 per share to existing stockholders and immediate dilution of $13.73 per share to new investors purchasing shares in this offering.

## MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS (EXCERPT)

**Overview.** We generate revenue primarily from subscriptions to our platform, which are generally one to three years in length and billed annually in advance. Subscription revenue was 88% of total revenue for the six months ended June 30, 2025. Professional services, principally implementation, made up the balance.

**Results of operations -- six months ended June 30, 2025 compared with 2024.** Revenue increased $15.4 million, or 35.5%, to $58.9 million, with $11.6 million of the increase attributable to new customers and $3.8 million to expansion by existing customers. Gross margin was 73.0% compared with 71.4%. Sales and marketing expense was $29.1 million, an increase of 22%.

**Liquidity.** As of June 30, 2025 we had cash and cash equivalents of $38.6 million. Net cash used in operating activities was $4.2 million for the six months ended June 30, 2025.
