## QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS ABOUT THE MEETING AND VOTING

**Why am I receiving these materials?** The Board of Directors is soliciting your proxy to vote at the annual meeting. You are receiving this proxy statement because you owned shares of common stock at the close of business on March 21, 2025, the record date.

**How many shares are entitled to vote?** 38,209,774 shares of common stock were outstanding on the record date. Each share is entitled to one vote on each matter.

**What is the quorum requirement?** A majority of the shares outstanding and entitled to vote, present in person or represented by proxy, constitutes a quorum.

**How do I vote?** You may vote by Internet at www.proxyvote.example, by telephone at 1-800-555-0147, by returning a signed proxy card, or during the meeting. If you hold shares through a broker, you must give voting instructions to your broker; brokers may not vote on Items 1, 3, 4 or 5 without your instructions.

**Can I change my vote?** Yes. You may revoke your proxy at any time before it is voted by submitting a later-dated proxy, by notifying the Corporate Secretary in writing or by voting at the meeting.

**What vote is required?** Directors are elected by a majority of votes cast in uncontested elections. Items 2 through 5 require the affirmative vote of a majority of shares present and entitled to vote. Abstentions have the effect of votes against; broker non-votes are not counted.
